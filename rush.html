How to play

Every junction starts with a long run-up through traffic. At the end, the junction is busy and the lights are red for you. Crash into it.

Heat multiplies your final score. Build it on the way in: near misses (closer is better), the oncoming lane, drifts and air. How fast you hit adds more.

Rush (Shift) is your boost. It fills the same way heat does, so the more you risk, the faster you go.

Once you crash, everything the crash reaches counts: each car hit by something you hit scores more, down a chain as long as the traffic keeps coming. Steer for a few seconds after impact to push your wreck into more trouble. Tankers explode.
