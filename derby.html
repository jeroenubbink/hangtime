Where to hit

Your nose is built to take it; doors aren't. T-bone them in the side for big damage, and keep your own sides away from their bumpers. Hits near a wheel can knock it clean off. Finish a car with a big side hit and it tears in half.

Engine & wheels

A battered nose loses power. A missing wheel drags and pulls. Real derby drivers often reverse into others to spare the engine; the game rewards it too.

Pickups

Repair, nitro, shield and spikes work at once. Shockwave and mines wait for E. Nitro also refills as you deal damage.

Events

Last Car Standing: the last car running wins. Brawl: three minutes, wrecked cars come back, most points wins. Wrecking Spree: the clock only stops for carnage. Championship: three rounds, points for placing.

Points

Damage, takedowns, spin-outs, wheels, ring-outs on the Sky Deck, revenge, and smashing cones and barrels. Keep scoring within 4 s to build a combo.
